Flagship

cpbit - Platform Gateway

Summarized directly from the platform architecture notes.

What it does

CPB-IT Platform Gateway (PG) is the central operations hub for CP Bangladesh. It unifies access management, attendance automation, QR initiatives, mobile applications, and administrative tooling into a single Laravel/Vue stack. The platform blends live dashboards, scheduled data sync jobs, messaging integrations, and hardware touchpoints to keep business units coordinated and informed.

  • Authentication & Authorization: Active Directory/LDAP sign-in, token-based sessions (Sanctum 4.1), fine-grained role permissions via middleware, multi-factor authentication, configurable session lifetimes and stateful domain management
  • Operations Dashboard: Personalized landing pages with quick-links, KPI cards, role-aware navigation for admin, super-admin, and end users, real-time metrics visualization, and customizable widgets
  • Attendance Automation: Automated synchronization with biometric attendance devices, COM-port readers for Windows hosts, centralized data warehousing with exports for payroll and compliance, multiple sync schedules (daily, morning, night, ad-hoc)
  • Mobile App Services: Comprehensive REST APIs for internal Android applications, content management, OTA (Over-The-Air) version management, APK version tracking and distribution
  • QR & Badge Management: Dynamic QR code generation and assignment, QR-based access control and event registration, mobile and web-based scanning integration, integrated reporting for QR code usage and access logs
  • iAccess & Ticketing: Digital service request management, automated workflow routing for different request categories, comprehensive audit trails, multi-level approval processes
  • Notification Pipelines: GP CMP SMS gateway integration for alerts and notifications, real-time Telegram broadcasts for system events, configurable email templates, multi-channel alerts
  • Document & Hardware Catalogues: Hardware lifecycle and inventory tracking, document and manual management system, comprehensive asset and hardware cataloguing
  • AI & Chatbot Services: Gemini/OpenAI integration for intelligent assistance, persistent chat history and context management, PDF indexing and search capabilities
